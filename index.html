Isle of Palms Bachelorette Party Guide

monday, october 12, 2026

Isle of Palms sits about 20–25 minutes from downtown Charleston, SC. You get big oceanfront rental homes, pool days and a quiet island base, plus easy access to King Street for a night out. Below: where to stay, where to eat and drink, the best bachelorette activities and ideas, and a weekend schedule that works for groups of 8–16.

Why Isle of Palms?

It's a family-friendly island, so the party happens at the house: pool, porch and beach. That makes it ideal for a relaxed bachelorette with one or two big nights in Charleston. Sullivan's Island is 10 minutes away for bars and casual food, and Mount Pleasant's Shem Creek is close for boat days.

Where Did You Stay?

Private beach houses

The go-to choice for groups. Search VRBO or Airbnb for 7–10 bedroom homes with a private pool, hot tub and direct beach access. Ask whether the pool can be heated (usually an extra fee) if you're going October–April. Large homes such as Belle Mer sleep big groups comfortably.

Resorts and hotels

  • Wild Dunes Resort: north end of the island; pools, spa, golf, beach service and resort-managed rental homes.
  • Seaside Inn: oceanfront hotel near Front Beach with bike rentals and beach chairs.
  • The Palms Oceanfront Hotel: steps from the sand, walkable to Front Beach bars.

How Did You Get There?

Fly into Charleston International (CHS), about 25–30 minutes away, and take an Uber or shuttle to the island. Charlotte is roughly a 3-hour drive, so driving groups can split cars and keep them for grocery runs.

How Did You Get Around?

Bring a few cars for errands, rent golf carts or bikes for the island, and Uber into Charleston. Rideshares can run $30–$100 each way at peak times, and late-night pickups on the island can take a while, so book return rides early or hire a driver for the night. A party bus usually isn't worth the cost for the short trip downtown.

Stocking the House

Do one big run on the way in. The Costco at Ashley Town Center sells beer, wine and liquor, so you can cover breakfast, charcuterie, bubbly and seltzers in one stop. Pick up smaller items at Publix at North Point in Mount Pleasant, about 15 minutes from the island.

Where to Eat on the Island

  • The Boathouse at Breach Inlet: seafood with water views at the Sullivan's Island bridge.
  • Acme Lowcountry Kitchen: Southern brunch and shrimp and grits.
  • The Obstinate Daughter (Sullivan's): pizza, pasta and seafood for a group dinner.
  • Home Team BBQ (Sullivan's): casual late lunch; expect a wait for big parties.
  • The Co-Op Frosé & Eatery: frosé flights and sandwiches, with shops on Sullivan's and Isle of Palms.

Where to Drink & Party on the Island

  • The Windjammer: beachfront bar with live music on Front Beach.
  • Coconut Joe's Beach Grill: rooftop cocktails over the ocean.
  • The Dinghy: laid-back island bar for a low-key night.

Downtown Charleston Night Out

Book dinner 4–6 weeks out; many restaurants ask large groups for a deposit or set menu.

  • Leon's Oyster Shop: fried chicken, oysters and an easy family-style option for big groups.
  • The Darling Oyster Bar: raw bar on King Street before barhopping.
  • Vintage Lounge: wine, bubbles and charcuterie on Upper King.
  • The Cocktail Club: craft cocktails with a rooftop.
  • Uptown Social: rooftop bar and a lively Upper King crowd.

Isle of Palms Bachelorette Activities

  • Beach day: rent chairs and umbrellas, or use the house path straight to the sand.
  • Boat day: Captain Cocktail Cruises runs BYOB party pontoons from Shem Creek with swim stops and floats.
  • Harbor cruise: the Daniel Island Ferry offers private charters and sunset trips.
  • Kayak or paddleboard: Coastal Expeditions rents boards and runs dolphin tours from Shem Creek.
  • Golf carts and bikes: the easiest way to cruise Front Beach and Sullivan's.
  • Golf or spa: both are on-site at Wild Dunes Resort.

Bachelorette Party Ideas for the House

  • Custom cups with letter stickers so everyone can find their drink.
  • Hangover kits: pain reliever, makeup wipes, electrolytes, snacks.
  • Prosecco pong with coupes instead of red cups.
  • Matching swim cover-ups or a white look for the bride.
  • Balloon name banner and an instant camera for the photo wall.
  • Pajama party with a lingerie shower: guests guess who gave each gift.

Night In: Bring the Show to the House

Since Isle of Palms nights are mostly spent at the house, plan one big night in. Pair the pajama party or lingerie shower with a private performance from Michael At Your Place, who comes to rentals across the Charleston area. No Ubers, no lines, and the bride stays the center of attention.

What Was the Weekend Schedule Like?

Thursday

Early arrivals stock the fridge and decorate. Everyone else trickles in; keep dinner easy with snacks and cocktails at the house.

Friday

Pool and beach day with a sandwich bar for lunch. Early dinner downtown at Leon's, then barhop Upper King.

Saturday

Sleep in. Frosé at The Co-Op, late lunch at Home Team BBQ or an afternoon boat. Night in: pizza, games, pajama party.

Sunday

Coffee, cleanup and a 10am checkout.

Quick Planning Tips

  • Best months: April–June and September–October. Winter is cheaper but the beach is windy.
  • Book early: big homes go 6–12 months out for spring weekends.
  • Check rules: Isle of Palms has beach rules on alcohol, glass and noise; keep the party at the house.
  • Rides home: schedule late-night pickups from downtown before you leave the island.
